# Audio Harbor — Marketing concept

Positioning for **audiophiles**, not for a feature-max player. Homepage lives in [`index.html`](index.html) (same layout language as [jj-breeze](https://github.com/petergerov/jj-breeze-auv3/tree/main/docs): light paper, quiet type, three-up grids, one dark close).

**Lead platform: macOS.** iPad and iPhone are the same product, honest about DAC limits.

---

## One-line

**Your folders. Your files. Bit-perfect when the DAC is ready.**

Tagline (locked in brand): *Local. Bit-perfect. Calm.*

Store subtitle: *Local audiophile player*

---

## Who it is for

| Audience | What they want | What we say |
|---|---|---|
| Mac + USB DAC | FLAC/DSD as the file is, hog mode, sample-rate match | Exclusive is a switch, not a religion. Shared is the daily default. |
| Vinyl/digital hybrid | Local library, no Roon tax, no streaming chrome | Open a folder. Hear the file. |
| iPhone / iPad on the train | Same catalogue language, headphones | Shared session. Exclusive and DoP are Mac. |
| Pine / Audirvana refugees | Power without a kitchen sink | Fewer controls. Better defaults. |

**Not for:** people who want Qobuz/Tidal, multi-room, or “768 kHz magic.” We do not pretend.

---

## Mac first, then the pocket

The brand is defined on the Mac: Exclusive, DoP, hog + rate match into a known DAC.

iPad and iPhone ship the **same** library, Deck, and rack — with platform-honest output:

| | Mac | iPad / iPhone |
|---|---|---|
| Catalogue, playlists, search | Yes | Yes |
| Shared playback | Yes (default) | Yes (the path) |
| Exclusive / DoP | Yes, USB DAC | No — Shared only |
| AUv3 rack | Yes (forces Shared) | Yes (forces Shared) |

Homepage copy must say this in one breath so audiophiles do not feel sold a fake Exclusive iPhone.

---

## Promise vs. proof

| Promise | Proof on the page |
|---|---|
| Local-first | Folders you pick. No account. No cloud of the files. |
| Honest quality | Shared / Exclusive / DoP explained in dummy language |
| Calm UI | Catalogue · Deck · Settings — nothing else competing |
| Bit-perfect when it matters | Exclusive + sample-rate match on Mac; badge when conversion is on |

**Do not claim:** bit-perfect on Bluetooth, AirPlay, or built-in speakers. Those stay Shared.

## Voice

Calm, precise, slightly analog. Short sentences. No neon “hi-res” badges.

**Use**

- Local. Bit-perfect. Calm.
- Start with Shared. Exclusive when your DAC is ready.
- Your folders. Your files. No cloud required.
- Features earn their place.

**Avoid**

- Upsampling as the hero
- DAC-model name-dropping as affiliation
- “Studio quality” / “pro audio suite”
- Feature matrices that look like Pine

8. **Quote** — one line they can repeat  
9. **Close** — GitHub now; Mac App Store when listed

Pre-release: **GitHub** as the honest CTA. Do not invent an App Store URL.
