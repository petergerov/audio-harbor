Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

Audio Harbor is a local music player. It is built to play files you already have, on devices you already own.

What we do not collect

  • No account, email, or sign-in
  • No analytics, advertising, or crash telemetry of your listening
  • No streaming services and no upload of your music to our servers
  • We do not sell data; there is no listening profile to sell

What stays on your device

Folder bookmarks, the catalogue index, playlists, output settings, and plugin state are stored on the Mac, iPad, or iPhone you use. The audio files remain in the folders you connect. We do not host them.

Permissions

The app asks for access to folders you choose, and for audio playback. On iPhone it may download iCloud Drive placeholders you explicitly open. That traffic is between you and Apple, not a Gerov server.
